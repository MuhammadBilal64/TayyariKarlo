# Interview visual direction — Ink & Graph (continued)

## Critique of first draft (fixed)

| Weak | Why | Stronger |
|------|-----|----------|
| “Set the brief” | Agency jargon, soft | **State the role** |
| “Mock interview” | Sounds fake/toy | **Oral round** |
| “How you did” | Casual report card | **Your score** |
| “Practice mode” + long infra note up top | Kills momentum | Short **Scripted practice** badge; one-line honesty under form |
| “Turn review” | Clinical | **Question by question** |
| “Start interview” | Fine but flat | **Begin interview** |
| “grilled on” | Try-hard slang | **press on** / plain labels |

## Direction
Same quiet study-studio system as auth/assessments: graph-paper, Instrument Serif + Sora, forest-teal signal. Interview = focused **oral desk**, not a chatbot.

## Voice
- Imperative titles (“State the role”, “Your score”).
- Short ledes; no marketing fluff.
- Honest about scripted vs live model without apologizing in the hero.

## Screens
- `setup.html` — role, stack, level → Begin interview
- `session.html` — progress rule, transcript, response box
- `results.html` — score hero, summary, question-by-question

## Routes (after visual approval)
- `/app/interviews` · `/app/interviews/:sessionId` · `/app/interviews/:sessionId/results`
