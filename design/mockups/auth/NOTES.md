# Auth visual direction — Ink & Graph

## Direction
Quiet study-studio feel: cool paper background with light graph lines, deep ink type, and a single forest-teal signal color. Display serif for brand/headlines; Sora for UI text.

## Why this fits TayyariKarlo
- Feels like focused prep, not a flashy SaaS dashboard.
- Trustworthy and calm for login/register.
- Distinctive without purple gradients, glass cards, or emerald-on-slate defaults.

## Screens
- `home.html` — brand-first hero, one lede, two CTAs
- `login.html` — split: brand story + form
- `register.html` — same system, checklist instead of marketing fluff
